<Note>
  To use {clause}, enable Cypher mutations with `enable_cypher_mutations: true` in your BloodHound [configuration file](https://github.com/SpecterOps/bloodhound-docs/blob/main//manage-bloodhound/bh-config#enable_cypher_mutations). Your account also needs the `GraphDBMutate` permission. These requirements apply to `CREATE`, `SET`, `REMOVE`, `DELETE`, and `DETACH DELETE`.
</Note>
